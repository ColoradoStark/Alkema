Privacy Policy

Last updated October 13, 2026

Alkema is a browser game at z3q.com. This page explains what information the game keeps about you, why, and what you can do about it. If anything here is unclear, email i@repulse.com.

Playing as a guest

You can play without an account. Your heroes (their look, name, quest progress and gold) and your sound setting are saved in your own browser's storage, on your device. They are not sent to our server, and you can remove them at any time by deleting heroes in My heroes or clearing your browser's site data.

If you create an account

An account lets you keep your heroes and play them on any device. We store:

  • Email address and a display name. If you sign up with an email address, we also store your password, but only as a one-way bcrypt hash; we never store or see the password itself.
  • If you sign in with Google or Discord: the account ID that service gives us, and the name and email address it shares (Google: name, email and whether the email is verified; Discord: username and email). We do not receive your Google or Discord password, and we do not post anything or read anything else from those accounts.
  • Your saved heroes: up to 10, each with its look, name, quest progress, gold and when it was last saved.
  • When the account was created and last signed in.

While you play

Alkema is multiplayer. While you are in the world, your hero's name, appearance, position and actions are sent to other players who are online, so they can see you. This is not stored after you leave.

Like most websites, our server and its web server record technical information when your browser connects, such as your IP address and the time of the request. We use it to keep the game running and secure, for example to slow down repeated wrong password attempts.

Cookies and browser storage

  • A sign-in cookie (alkema_session) keeps you signed in. It is only set if you have an account, it can't be read by the page's scripts or sent by other websites, and it expires after 60 days.
  • A short-lived cookie is used for about ten minutes while you sign in with Google or Discord, to protect that step.
  • Browser storage holds guest heroes, which hero you last played, and your sound setting.

We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, and we do not show ads.

How we use your information

Only to run the game: to sign you in, save and load your heroes, show your hero to other players, and keep the service secure. We do not sell, rent or trade your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who else is involved

  • Our hosting provider (OVH) runs the server where the game and accounts are stored.
  • Google and Discord, only if you choose to sign in with them. Their own privacy policies apply to your accounts with them.

We would disclose information only if required by law.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your account and saved heroes for as long as you have an account. You can delete individual heroes at any time in My heroes. To delete your account and everything stored with it, email i@repulse.com from the address on your account, and we will remove it. You can also ask us for a copy of what we hold about you.

Security

Passwords are hashed, the connection to z3q.com is encrypted (HTTPS), and only your account can read your saves. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Children

Alkema is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions or requests: i@repulse.com.
