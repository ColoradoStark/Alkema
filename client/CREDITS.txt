======================================================================
BATTLES
======================================================================

The foes you fight round Riverhold are The Battle for Wesnoth's
(https://www.wesnoth.org) unit art, by the Wesnoth unit artists (the
full list of contributors is in data/core/about.cfg of the Wesnoth
repository): the Orcish Grunt; the Goblin Pillager, Spearman, Wolf
Rider, Goblin Knight and Direwolf Rider; the Skeleton, Skeleton Rider, Bone Knight, Ghost,
Shadow and Nightgaunt; and on the coast west of where you start, the
Orcish Archer, Warrior and Warlord with grunts and goblin spearmen. Their
standing, idle, walking, attack, defend and death frames are used, scaled
up a quarter, with their team colour mapped to LPC cloth (maroon for orcs
and goblins, purple for the undead; the grunt's, archer's, warrior's and
warlord's every colour mapped to the LPC character palettes, and the
grunt's dropped sword drawn into its last death frames).
  License: GNU GPL version 2 or later
  https://github.com/wesnoth/wesnoth/tree/master/data/core/images/units
The foes in Gloomwater Grotto are Duelyst's Vanar (the Snowchaser, Ice
Hornet, Crystal Beetle, Water Elemental, Kindred Hunter and Water Bear),
by Counterplay Games' artists, released by Open Duelyst under CC0. Their
breathing, idle, run, attack, hit and death frames are used, recoloured
to match the brightness and saturation of the LPC character art; one copy of each is also matched to the LPC
palettes. The Snowchaser, Ice Hornet, Crystal Beetle, Water Elemental,
Kindred Hunter and Water Bear are scaled up a quarter.
  License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication)
  https://github.com/open-duelyst/duelyst/tree/main/app/resources/units
The cave battle map is drawn with Gloomwater Grotto's art (credited below:
Wesnoth's cave floors and rough wall and mushroom groves, the LPC atlas's
boulders, mushrooms and reeds, Sharm's bones and skulls).
The battle maps are laid out from the same Wesnoth grass, snow and
trees as the world map (below), with Wesnoth's dirt and its ground
decorations (flowers, stones, rocks, mushrooms, snow) by its terrain
artists.
  License: GNU GPL version 2 or later
  https://github.com/wesnoth/wesnoth/tree/master/data/core/images/terrain/flat
  https://github.com/wesnoth/wesnoth/tree/master/data/core/images/terrain/embellishments
Where foes and the party start on a battle map follows Ultima IV's
combat maps (Origin Systems; freeware from Electronic Arts): the
start positions only, no art or data from it.

======================================================================
OVERWORLD MOUNTAINS, FORESTS, GRASS, SAND AND SNOW
======================================================================

The mountains, the forests (the far north's snow-laden woods among
them), the grass, the beaches, the desert and the snowfields on
the world map are the painted
terrain of The Battle for Wesnoth (https://www.wesnoth.org), by the
Wesnoth terrain artists, chiefly Hogne Håskjold (frame), terrain art
director, who made much of its terrain and especially the mountains;
the desert mountains are by doofus-01.
  License: GNU GPL version 2 or later
  https://github.com/wesnoth/wesnoth/tree/master/data/core/images/terrain/mountains
  https://github.com/wesnoth/wesnoth/tree/master/data/core/images/terrain/forest
  https://github.com/wesnoth/wesnoth/tree/master/data/core/images/terrain/grass
  https://github.com/wesnoth/wesnoth/tree/master/data/core/images/terrain/sand
  https://github.com/wesnoth/wesnoth/tree/master/data/core/images/terrain/frozen

Each multi-hex mountain was reassembled from its per-hex slices and
its hex-cut edges dissolved into the grass; single trees were cut out
of the sparse woods; and all their colours were mapped to the game's
palette. The grass and sand hexes were laid side by side into fields
that repeat seamlessly across the square map, their edges shaped by
the LPC terrain tiles.

generated by AI image models (Krea 2 by Krea, then recoloured with
Krea's K3 Edit to match the character's in-game sprite), from a
description of that character as the character generator made them.
Wren's voiced, animated clips (one per line she speaks) are AI-generated
too: MiniMax H3 animates her portrait and speaks her written lines.

GLOOMWATER GROTTO (THE DUNGEON)
======================================================================

The grotto's levels (tools/dungeongen.py) blend The Battle for Wesnoth's
terrain with LPC things, as the battle maps do:

wesnoth-cave/ (the cave floor, the earthy floor, the dark flagstones and the
rough cave wall) and wesnoth-mushrooms/ (the mushroom groves, on dry ground and
in the water): The Battle for Wesnoth's terrain art, taken unchanged from
data/core/images/terrain/cave and data/core/images/terrain/forest
(https://github.com/wesnoth/wesnoth), by the Wesnoth terrain artists (the
full list of contributors is in data/core/about.cfg of the Wesnoth
repository). The stones, rocks and little mushrooms strewn on the floor are
Wesnoth's embellishments (see BATTLES).
  License: GNU GPL version 2 or later

ladder.png: from the LPC Base Assets' mountains.png by Lanea Zimmerman
(Sharm).
  License: CC-BY-SA 3.0 / GPL 3.0
  https://opengameart.org/content/liberated-pixel-cup-lpc-base-assets-sprites-map-tiles

The iron gate, the stone font, the pillars, the mossy pillars, the great
carved head, the boulder, the waterfall (darkened for the cave), the reeds,
the gravestones and the planks of the steps down are from the LPC atlas
(tools/worldgen-sources/terrain_atlas.png: Lanea Zimmerman (Sharm), Jetrel,
Daniel Eddeland and others; credits in tools/worldgen-sources/ATLAS-CREDITS.txt).
The chests, the dock, the bridge's planks and rails, the rowboat, the crates
and barrels, the skulls and bones are the towns' (tools/towngen-sources,
credits in TOWN-CREDITS.txt).
