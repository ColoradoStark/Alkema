painterly/ holds the 256 px originals used (each icon's brightest version, the pack's file
names without the part prefix); tools/spellgen.py scales them to 64 px for the game.
painterly/tiers/ holds, scaled to 128 px and otherwise unchanged, the pack's two dimmer versions of
each of those icons and one other colour of each (a spell's tiers on the Magic tab).

Spell effects: The Battle for Wesnoth (https://www.wesnoth.org)
wesnoth-fx/ holds frames taken unchanged from data/core/images/projectiles and
data/core/images/halo (fireball and its impact, fire bursts, flame burst, the
fire missile, druid healing, leadership flare, teleport, dark magic, the lightning bolts and the white missile's impact; the faerie fire halo, for the elven glow: tools/elven_glow.py) in
https://github.com/wesnoth/wesnoth, by the Wesnoth artists (the full list of
contributors is in data/core/about.cfg).
