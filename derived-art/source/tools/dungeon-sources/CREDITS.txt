GLOOMWATER GROTTO (THE DUNGEON)
======================================================================

The grotto's levels (tools/dungeongen.py) blend The Battle for Wesnoth's
terrain with LPC things, as the world map does:

wesnoth-cave/ (the cave floor, the earthy floor, the dark flagstones and the
rough cave wall) and wesnoth-mushrooms/ (the mushroom groves, on dry ground and
in the water): The Battle for Wesnoth's terrain art, taken unchanged from
data/core/images/terrain/cave and data/core/images/terrain/forest
(https://github.com/wesnoth/wesnoth), by the Wesnoth terrain artists (the
full list of contributors is in data/core/about.cfg of the Wesnoth
repository). The stones, rocks and little mushrooms strewn on the floor are
Wesnoth's embellishments (tools/battle-sources/wesnoth-embellishments).
  License: GNU GPL version 2 or later

ladder.png: from the LPC Base Assets' mountains.png by Lanea Zimmerman
(Sharm).
  License: CC-BY-SA 3.0 / GPL 3.0
  https://opengameart.org/content/liberated-pixel-cup-lpc-base-assets-sprites-map-tiles

The iron gate, the stone font, the pillars, the mossy pillars, the great
carved head, the boulder, the waterfall (darkened for the cave), the reeds,
the gravestones and the planks of the steps down are from the LPC atlas
(tools/worldgen-sources/terrain_atlas.png: Lanea Zimmerman (Sharm), Jetrel,
Daniel Eddeland and others; credits in tools/worldgen-sources/ATLAS-CREDITS.txt).
The chests, the dock, the bridge's planks and rails, the rowboat, the crates
and barrels, the skulls and bones are the towns' (tools/towngen-sources,
credits in TOWN-CREDITS.txt).
