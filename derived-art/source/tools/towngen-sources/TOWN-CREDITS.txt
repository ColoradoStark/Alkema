RIVERHOLD (THE TOWN)
======================================================================

The town of Riverhold is put together from these LPC sets:

"[LPC] Thatched-roof Cottage" by bluecarrot16, based on the LPC Base
Assets by Lanea Zimmerman (Sharm) and Daniel Armstrong (HughSpectrum),
and the LPC art entry by Casper Nilsson.
  License: CC-BY-SA 3.0 / GPL 3.0
  https://opengameart.org/content/lpc-thatched-roof-cottage

"[LPC] Roofs" by bluecarrot16, Lanea Zimmerman (Sharm), Michele Bucelli
(Buch), Casper Nilsson, Xenodora, keith karnage, NaRNeRZz, and Talon
(Talosaurus).
  License: CC-BY-SA 3.0 / GPL 3.0
  https://opengameart.org/content/lpc-roofs

"LPC Windows & Doors" by bluecarrot16, Lanea Zimmerman (Sharm), Daniel
Armstrong (HughSpectrum), Casper Nilsson, Anamaris, Krusmira, Keith
Karnage, and Guido Bos.
  License: CC-BY-SA 3.0 / GPL 3.0+
  https://opengameart.org/content/lpc-windows-doors

"[LPC] Terrains" by bluecarrot16, Lanea Zimmerman (Sharm), Daniel
Eddeland (Daneeklu), Richard Kettering (Jetrel), Zachariah Husiar
(Zabin), Hyptosis, Casper Nilsson, Buko Studios, Nushio, ZaPaper,

"[LPC] Medieval Village Decorations" by bluecarrot16, after Reemax
([LPC] Hanging signs; Signposts, graves, line cloths and scare crow;

  License: CC-BY-SA 3.0 / GPL 3.0
  https://opengameart.org/content/lpc-tile-atlas

The buildings were assembled from these parts at the sizes the town
plan gives them; the shop names are lettered in Alagard by Hewett Tsoi.

The LPC tile atlas's own credits are in ../worldgen-sources/ATLAS-CREDITS.txt.
town-terrain.png is cut from [LPC] Terrains (terrain-map-v8.png) and [LPC] Bricks;
town-terrain.json gives each tile's corner terrains.

chests.png (the treasure chests behind the shopkeepers' counters):
  from the LPC Base Assets by Lanea Zimmerman (Sharm), CC-BY-SA 3.0 / GPL 3.0 / OGA-BY 3.0.

TUMBLEDOWN AND GULL'S REST (THE NORTHERN AND HARBOUR VILLAGES)
======================================================================

The snow, trodden snow, ice and icy shore tiles in town-terrain.png were added
from "[LPC] Terrains" (above) by tools/town_terrain.py.

winter-trees.png (the snow-laden pines and the chimney in the snow) and
winter-roof.png (the snowed-up roof): "LPC Winter Tiles" by Demetrius, based
on the LPC: Modified Base Tiles by Lanea Zimmerman (Sharm) and William Thompson.
  License: CC-BY 3.0 / OGA-BY 3.0 / CC-BY-SA 3.0 / GPL 3.0
  https://opengameart.org/content/lpc-winter-tiles

seasons-trees.png (the bare and the snow-dusted winter trees),
seasons-trunks.png (stumps), seasons-bushes.png (the frosted bush) and
seasons-rocks.png: "[LPC Revised] 4-Season Terrain" by Eliza Wyatt
(DeathsDarling), after Lanea Zimmerman (Sharm) and Hyptosis.
  License: OGA-BY 3.0 (per-piece credits in CREDITS-seasons.txt)
  https://opengameart.org/content/lpc-revised-4-season-terrain

dock.png: "Dock tileset" by Reid.
  License: CC-BY-SA 3.0
  https://opengameart.org/content/dock-tileset

rowboat.png: the LPC rowboat by Daniel Eddeland (daneeklu), recoloured by
AntumDeluge ("LPC Rowboat Recolor").
  License: CC-BY-SA 3.0 / GPL 3.0
  https://opengameart.org/content/lpc-rowboat-recolor

The fish, crabs, mussels and oysters are from "[LPC] Foods" (food.png, above).
The fishermen's rod and the woodcutters' axe are from "[LPC] Hand Tools" by
bluecarrot16 and others, drawn by the character generator.


CASTLE DUNMERE
======================================================================

Castle Dunmere (tools/castlegen.py) is put together from these LPC sets,
besides the town's own (above):

castle-walls.png, castle-outside.png and castle-floors.png (the curtain
walls and their battlements, the gate arch, the round towers under their
cones, the keep's tall windows, pilasters and double door, the tiled
floor of the treasure room): the castle work in the LPC Base Assets by
Daniel Armstrong (HughSpectrum).
bridges.png (the drawbridge's planks and rails): the LPC Base Assets by
Lanea Zimmerman (Sharm).
  License: CC-BY-SA 3.0 / GPL 3.0
  https://opengameart.org/content/liberated-pixel-cup-lpc-base-assets-sprites-map-tiles

castle-interior.png (the castle rooms' walls, cornice, damask and
wainscot, the parquet and slate floors, the red rug, the dais, the royal
bed, the great hearth, the candelabra, the columns, the clock, the sofa
and the potted tree, the portraits, the landscape and the arched
windows): "LPC: Interior Castle Tiles" by Lanea Zimmerman (Sharm).
  License: CC-BY 3.0 / CC-BY-SA 3.0 / GPL 3.0 / OGA-BY 3.0
  https://opengameart.org/content/lpc-interior-castle-tiles

city-inside.png (the throne, the braziers, the red banners, the bust and
its column, the cells' bars, the cloth-covered tables, the cupboard,
the mirror, the chairs, the cobbled and the yellow stone floors): "[LPC] City inside"
by Reemax, Tuomo Untinen, Lanea Zimmerman (Sharm), Daniel Armstrong
(HughSpectrum) and Guido Bos (per-piece credits in its credits.txt: the
throne, braziers, banners, bust and cell walls are Tuomo Untinen's).
  License: CC-BY-SA 3.0 / GPL 3.0 / GPL 2.0
  https://opengameart.org/content/lpc-city-inside

dungeon-things.png (the chains, the cauldron, the rough table and
stools): "[LPC] Dungeon Elements" by Lanea Zimmerman (Sharm),
contributed by William.Thompsonj.
  License: CC-BY 3.0 / GPL 3.0 / GPL 2.0 / OGA-BY 3.0
  https://opengameart.org/content/lpc-dungeon-elements
