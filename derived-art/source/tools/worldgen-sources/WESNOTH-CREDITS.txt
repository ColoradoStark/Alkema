Mountains, forests, grass, sand and snow: The Battle for Wesnoth
=============================================

The images in wesnoth-mountains/, wesnoth-forest/ and wesnoth-ground/ are the
mountain, forest (snowy woods included), grass, sand and snow terrain art of The
Battle for Wesnoth (https://www.wesnoth.org), taken unchanged from
data/core/images/terrain/mountains, .../terrain/forest, .../terrain/grass,
.../terrain/sand and .../terrain/frozen in
https://github.com/wesnoth/wesnoth (wesnoth-ground's files are named <dir>-<file>).

Artists: the Wesnoth terrain artists, chiefly Hogne Håskjold (frame), terrain
art director, who made much of the current terrain and especially the
mountains; the desert ("dry") mountains are by doofus-01. The full list of
contributors is in data/core/about.cfg of the Wesnoth repository.

License: GNU GPL version 2 or later (https://www.gnu.org/licenses/old-licenses/gpl-2.0.html).

In this game the mountains are reassembled from their per-hex slices and their
hex-cut edges dissolved into the grass, single trees are cut out of the sparse
woods, and all their colours are mapped to the game's palette
(tools/worldart.py: wesnoth_mountain, trees); the grass and sand hexes are
laid out into seamless repeating fields that fill the ground tiles
(tools/worldgen.py: hex_field); the results are in client/assets/world.
