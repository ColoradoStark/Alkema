Weapon icons: Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup tiles
=============================================

These 32x32 tiles are from the Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup tile set, as released
under CC0 by the Crawl tiles project (releases/Ancient/Oct-5-2010/item/weapon
in https://github.com/crawl/tiles), by the DCSS artists (list:
https://github.com/crawl/tiles/blob/master/ARTISTS.md). License: CC0 1.0
(public domain dedication), http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

longbow1-2015.png is longbow1.png from the same project's Nov-2015 release
(releases/Nov-2015/item/weapon/ranged), also CC0.

The attack button's long sword (client/assets/ui/long_sword_1_old.png until
October 2026) is this set's long_sword1.png. tools/weapon_icons.py lays them
side by side, unchanged, in client/assets/ui/weapons.png, and says which
weapon shows which (the attack button shows the weapon you carry).

The blue button's scroll (client/assets/ui/scroll_old.png, shown until a
spell's painting takes its place) is this release's item/scroll/scroll.png,
unchanged, also CC0.
