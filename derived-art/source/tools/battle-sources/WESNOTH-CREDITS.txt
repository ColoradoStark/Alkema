Foes and ground decorations: The Battle for Wesnoth
=================================================================

The images in wesnoth-embellishments/ (flowers, stones, rocks, mushrooms, snow) are Wesnoth's terrain art,
taken unchanged from data/core/images/terrain/embellishments, by the Wesnoth terrain artists; tools/dungeongen.py
strews them in Gloomwater Grotto.

The wesnoth-<foe>/ folders hold the units' frames from The Battle for Wesnoth
(https://www.wesnoth.org), taken unchanged from data/core/images/units (orcs, goblins, elves-wood) in
https://github.com/wesnoth/wesnoth, by the Wesnoth unit artists, their animation timings from
data/core/units (commit cad4edaccdb0a1e1f430afc7adcab07b3fd79671 for those added in November 2026): the
Orcish Grunt, Archer, Warrior and Warlord and the Goblin Spearman, for the coast west of the old start; the
Giant Rat, Ruffian, Woodsman, Footpad, Poacher, Goblin Impaler and Goblin Rouser for the Saltmere Point; the
Elder Wose and Elvish Sorceress guarding the great wood round it. The Elvish Shaman, Druid, Enchantress, Shyde and Sylph
(data/core/images/units/elves-wood, their timings from data/core/units/elves, commit
bc78728314ea0403b0dbeecb488845fdc5822f55) for the Southern Forest. The Fencer, Cavalryman, Horseman and Knight
(data/core/images/units/human-loyalists, their timings from data/core/units/humans, commit
eeea9ac0361a6e98f1f6e85b746f15bd65210586) for the Market Fields, in Dunmere's navy.
tools/battlegen.py recolours their team colour (to LPC cloth); the grunt's colours are all
mapped to the LPC character palettes (team colour to maroon cloth, iron to LPC iron and steel, leather to
LPC leather), and its dropped sword is drawn into its last death frames.

Artists: the Wesnoth terrain and unit artists. The full list of contributors
is in data/core/about.cfg of the Wesnoth repository.
