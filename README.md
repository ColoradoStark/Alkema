# Alkema

A character creator and multiplayer game demo built on [Liberated Pixel Cup](https://lpc.opengameart.org) sprites. It can create unlimited characters with deep aesthetic customization, equip weapons and armor, you can also test them in a live multiplayer environment.

## Character Creator

### Races and Body Types

30 playable races across 8 body types. Each race has its own forced features (elf ears, demon wings, furry tails), skin color palettes, and equipment restrictions.

**Races** — Human, Elf, Grey Elf, Orc, Goblin, Troll, Minotaur, Lizard, Dragonblood, Angel, Demon, Vampire, Skeleton, Zombie, Alien, and more — plus Fey variants (Pixie, Sylph, Dark) and Furry variants (Cat, Fox, Wolf, Bunny).

**Body types** — Male, Female, Muscular, Teen, Child, Pregnant, Skeleton, Zombie.

### Classes and Equipment

13 character classes, each with preferred weapons, armor, and equipment rules.

**Classes** — Warrior, Mage, Ranger, Thief, Pirate, Cleric, Noble, Guard, Merchant, Peasant, and age-based starter classes.

**Armor weights** — Heavy, Normal, Light, Formal, Topless, and Nude — each controlling which clothing and accessories are available.

### Aesthetic Customization

657 items across 104 categories. Mix and match:

- **91 hairstyles** with color variants
- **52 hats** with trims, buckles, and overlays
- **9 shields** with 49 heraldic patterns, paint, and trim layers
- **Cosmetics** — beards, mustaches, earrings, necklaces, hair extensions, scars, wounds
- **Race features** — wings, tails, horns, fins, furry ears
- **10 color palettes** that coordinate fabrics, accents, and metals across all equipped items

### Name Generation

Race-aware fantasy name generator produces unique names based on race and gender — humans get different naming conventions than orcs, fey, demons, or furry races.

## Weapons and Combat

### 36 Weapons

Swords, axes, polearms, daggers, bows, crossbows, slingshots, staves, wands, and more. Each weapon maps to the correct attack animation:

- **Swords and blades** → Slash (with oversized 128px or 192px attack frames)
- **Polearms and spears** → Thrust (oversized)
- **Bows, crossbows, slingshots** → Shoot (with projectile spawning)
- **Staves and wands** → Thrust or Slash depending on weapon

### Hit Detection

Server-side collision using oriented bounding boxes (OBB) computed per-frame from actual weapon sprite alpha data. Melee hits check point-in-OBB, ranged weapons spawn projectiles with their own collision. Hit cooldowns prevent multi-hits per attack swing.

### 15 Animations

Walk, Run, Idle, Combat Idle, Slash, Thrust, Spellcast, Shoot, Backslash, Halfslash, Jump, Sit, Emote, Hurt — all in 4 directions. Weapons with oversized sprites (128px, 192px) get larger attack frames automatically.

## Playing: Create Your Hero

Every session opens in a character creator built for one-thumb play on a phone:

- Pick a body (Male / Female), a race (Human, Elf, Furry, Fey) and a class (Warrior, Mage, Ranger, Thief, Cleric), or tap the **Random New Hero** die
- The die rolls a whole new hero every time (body, race, class, look and name), and so does every visit
- Random heroes get natural skin tones; fur, lavender and other race colors are yours to choose in **Customize look**, along with race kind (e.g. Cat, Fox, Wolf, Bunny), hair style, hair color and outfit colors
- A live preview walks your hero through the town and shows off only the moves their gear fully supports; rotate them to see every side
- Between walks, your hero spars a skeleton, goblin or orc with their class's real attacks: sword swings, arrows, magic bolts. Hits match what was struck (skeletons rattle, arrows throw splinters and a drifting feather, shields spark, spells scatter glints), and the gold won spills out as spinning coins that bounce, settle and fly into your purse, each with a rising chime
- Each class starts with a humble kit (e.g. a warrior gets legion armor, an arming sword and a wooden shield), revealed from a treasure chest when you press **Play**
- **Play** is always one tap away

Other races and classes are reserved for NPCs and enemies.

## Accounts and Saved Heroes

- Play straight away as a guest; a user icon in the corner (top right in the creator, at the end of the top bar in the world) opens your account menu
- Every hero you play is saved, with their look, name, quest progress and gold: up to 10 heroes, listed under **My heroes**, where you can switch hero or delete one
- Guests' heroes are saved in the browser; create an account with your email and a password to keep them, and your guest heroes come with you
- Coming back shows the hero you last played, with **Random New Hero** still one tap away
- **About** at the bottom of the account menu links to the About us, Contact, Terms of service and Privacy policy pages

## Sound and Music

- Music that follows you: a heroic overture in the character creator, then a rotating playlist of town and countryside tracks in the world, crossfading between moods
- Every tap, pick and roll has a sound: dice rattle for **Random New Hero**, each class answers with its weapon (a sword drawn, a bow twang, a spell), pages turn in **Customize look**
- The creator's sparring bouts and the world's fights are fully voiced: whooshes, bone clacks, blocks, monster cries, a victory jingle and coins
- The starter chest thuds down, creaks open and chimes for each item
- A speaker button cycles sound on, music off, or silent, and remembers your choice

## Multiplayer

Real-time multiplayer via Socket.io. Players share one overworld, built from LPC tiles:

- A 512×512-tile map in the spirit of the classic Ultima games: a continent, one large island and an arc of three small isles off the south-west coast (a long ridge, a crescent round a lagoon, a teardrop trailing an islet) in an animated ocean
- A great range of snow-capped peaks (The Battle for Wesnoth's hand-painted mountains, brought into the LPC palette: single peaks at the edges of a range, long snowy ridges in its heart, sandy ones by the desert) walls off the north, crossed by a single pass; more ranges run along the west coast, the south-east and the big island
- Two rivers that meet and run to the sea, opening into a broad estuary, crossed by three great stone bridges with arched spans and bannered pylons
- A long, winding arm of the continent (a narrow neck, a wooded headland, a hooked spur round a bay with a fishing village) broad where it leaves the mainland, reaches out toward the big island and ends across a narrow strait from the cursed city of Morvath: stand at its tip and the view pans across the water to the city, but only a ship will take you there
- Cities, villages, castles and dungeons shown as hand-drawn map icons in the LPC style (a walled city with paved streets and a cathedral spire, a castle round a flagstone courtyard, a village among its fields, a moat before the castle gate, a stone portal set into the foot of a Wesnoth mountain), each placed for a reason: every town on water (where the rivers meet, at the ends of the bridges, a port at the river mouth, harbours on the coast, a port for the desert, and where the pass road meets the northern sea), villages on the strips of shore below the western mountains, and every castle where it guards something (the foot of the pass, the coast road through the south-eastern hills, the northern sea cliffs, the island's ridge); caves sit in the mountain walls, and one on an isle just off the south-west shore, its mouth in sight across the channel from the beach. 8 cities, 5 villages, 4 castles and 8 dungeons in all
- The further a city or castle lies off the beaten track, the darker it is: those near the start are bright and bannered, then plain and grey, then grim with blood-red banners and few lights, and the most remote (beyond the northern range and on the great island) are black, broken ruins with a sickly light in their windows and a mist creeping through their streets
- Forests of The Battle for Wesnoth's painted trees, in the game's greens: broadleaf and pine woods, dense groves in their depths, great old trees, bushes and a touch of autumn, with pine woods thick at the foot of every range and climbing its lower slopes; you pass in front of and behind them, and a tree you stand behind thins so you can still see yourself
- Deep in a forest the light fails: the further in you go, the closer the dark comes, until you can see only a few steps around you
- One village lies hidden in a clearing at the heart of the great wood; it isn't on the minimap and its name appears only once you find it
- Walk up to a place and you're asked whether to enter it. Riverhold, where new heroes start, can be entered (the other places can't yet): a market town in the manner of Ultima III where the two rivers meet
  - A cobbled square with a well and market stalls, ringed by buildings of every size: the Weaponsmith, the Armourer, the Healer's stone hall and the Apothecary (each with its name painted over the door and a hanging sign), The Ferryman's Rest inn, the stables, townhouses and thatched cottages
  - Built from LPC timber-framed walls, thatched and tiled roofs, doors, windows and town props, put together at whatever size each building needs
  - Walk up into a door and you're inside: 13 of the buildings have rooms (14 with the inn's upper floor) furnished from the LPC house interior set, by what they are for: swords and spear racks behind the weaponsmith's counter, helmets and breastplates at the armourer's, beds in the healer's hall, a still and shelves of books and bottles at the apothecary, a bar and a fireplace in the inn's taproom with a staircase up to a floor of guest beds, and beds, hearths, clocks and cupboards in the homes. Walk down out of the door to go back into the street
  - Places off the main street to find: a back alley behind the smithy where a hooded stranger lurks (and a fence's den behind a barred door), a graveyard behind the healer's hall, a riverside walk, a pier out into the river, and a footbridge over the creek to a hermit's hut and a fisherwoman's shack on the outskirts
  - Townsfolk made by the character generator: the shopkeepers behind their counters, drinkers at the inn, a patient at the healer's, people at home in their houses, a ferryman, a gravedigger and more keep their places and turn to face you as you come up; others wander the square and the streets
  - The town watch: twelve guards in one uniform (chainmail, a Norman helm, a spear and a tattered cloak; each with their own face) at the gate, round the square, at the healer's, the footbridge, the pier and the graveyard, three on patrol and one off duty at the inn
  - Story mode: walk into someone and the world waits while they speak, like a visual novel: their words, a line at a time, typed out in a box where the controls were, with their name, and above it, filling the screen, an anime-style painting of them where they stand, recoloured to match their sprite (AI-generated from their character data), or for anyone without one a crisp close-up of their own sprite. Space, Enter or a tap moves the story on; ◀ (or Backspace) goes back a line and LOG (or H) shows everything said so far; some ask you a question, answered Yes or No
  - Wren, an elf thief just inside Riverhold's gate, wants to get to Stonebridge but daren't travel the river road alone, and asks to come with you. She speaks: every line she says comes with a short voiced, animated clip of her saying it (AI-generated from her portrait), played over her painting as the line appears, and again if you go back to it
  - In a town or a room you see only the players who are there with you; walk out through the gate and you're back on the overworld
- A galleon waits at anchor off the port: walk into it to board and it sails like a ship, turning toward where you steer, gathering speed slowly, coasting, riding the swell (it yaws, surges and pitches, so it never runs ruler-straight), with a V of foam behind it. Sail the sea to reach the islands and steer into land to step ashore; the ship waits where you leave it. Other players see your ship
- Mountains, place icons and water block your way on foot; you can walk through the trees
- The world wraps around: walk off the east edge and you arrive at the west, and north joins south

Also in the world:

- Live character movement and animation sync
- Attack and spellcast broadcasting
- Equipment changes reflected in real time
- Player join/leave events
- Mobile-responsive layout with touch controls
- A round minimap showing the land around you, other players, and the way to your current quest
- Starter quests that teach the controls, then lead you to a river bridge, a village, a castle and a dungeon's mouth; every new hero starts them fresh
- A short cooldown on the ability button, shown as a filling gold ring
- Warm golden-hour light over the world, matching the character creator's stage, where motes drift in the light and a spotlight falls on your hero while you customize

## Art Attribution

Every LPC sprite asset is tracked to its original artist and license. The API generates per-character credit files listing all contributing artists, their licenses (CC-BY-SA, CC-BY, CC0, OGA-BY, GPL), and source URLs — so attribution stays accurate no matter which items a character uses. 71 artists credited across the current asset library.

## API

A REST API powers all character generation. Full interactive docs at `/docs` (Swagger UI).

| Endpoint | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| `POST /generate-sprite` | Generate a character spritesheet PNG from item selections |
| `GET /random-character` | Generate a random character with race/class/armor rules; supports starter kits, seeds and look overrides |
| `GET /random-character/sprite` | Random character as a PNG |
| `POST /generate-gif` | Animated GIF promo image from selections |
| `GET /random-character/gif` | Random character as an animated GIF |
| `POST /character-credits` | Per-character artist attribution file |
| `GET /items`, `GET /items/{category}` | Browse the item database |
| `POST /available-options` | Items compatible with current selections |
| `GET /classes`, `GET /rules` | Character class and generation rule docs |
| `GET /player-options` | Races, classes and looks available to players, with color swatches |
| `GET /random-name` | A race-appropriate fantasy name |
| `GET /weapon-hitboxes` | Weapon hitbox data for all melee weapons |
| `POST /auth/register`, `POST /auth/login`, `POST /auth/logout`, `GET /auth/me` | Player accounts (email and password; session cookie) |
| `GET /auth/providers` | Which sign-in options are available |
| `GET /saves`, `PUT /saves/{id}`, `DELETE /saves/{id}`, `POST /saves/import` | A signed-in player's saved heroes (up to 10) |

## Tech Stack

- **FastAPI** + **Pillow** — Sprite composition and REST API
- **PostgreSQL** + **SQLAlchemy** — Item database with tag/layer relationships
- **MongoDB** — Game state and player persistence
- **Phaser 3** — Browser game engine
- **Socket.io** — Real-time multiplayer
- **Docker Compose** — Full stack orchestration with auto-deploy
- **Vite** — Client build tool
